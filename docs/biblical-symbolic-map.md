# Biblical Symbolic Map

## Scope

This document records the biblical patterns that inspired the framework. It distinguishes source material from later symbolic synthesis.

## Jesus / Yeshua

The framework begins with the meaning associated with the name Jesus/Yeshua: "The LORD saves" or "God saves." The framework uses "saving" functionally as preservation, restoration, and recovery rather than as a claim that a secular model replaces Christian theology.

## Seven Petitions

The Lord's Prayer is used as a seven-part structural inspiration:

1. Hallowed be thy name -> Create & Sustain
2. Thy kingdom come -> Restore & Care
3. Thy will be done -> Develop & Teach
4. Give us this day our daily bread -> Provide & Belong
5. Forgive us our debts/trespasses -> Account & Repair
6. Lead us not into temptation -> Direct & Restrain
7. Deliver us from evil -> Protect & Preserve

The framework treats these as a symbolic correspondence. The biblical text itself does not present the modern occupational or engineering categories in this form.

## Adam to Enoch

Genesis 5 gives the sequence:

Adam -> Seth -> Enosh -> Kenan -> Mahalalel -> Jared -> Enoch

The framework uses working associations based on common discussions of name meanings:

- Adam -> humanity/man/earth
- Seth -> appointed/established
- Enosh -> mortal/vulnerable human
- Kenan -> uncertain; symbolic provision/belonging association is a framework choice
- Mahalalel -> praise/value/blessedness
- Jared -> descent/coming down
- Enoch -> dedicated/initiated/teaching

Because name etymologies can be uncertain or disputed, these should be treated as interpretive associations.

## Seven-Generation Teaching Sequence

The resulting working sequence is:

Humanity -> Appointed -> Mortal -> Provision -> Value -> Descent -> Dedicated Teaching

And:

Create & Sustain -> Restore & Care -> Develop & Teach -> Provide & Belong -> Account & Repair -> Direct & Restrain -> Protect & Preserve

The phrase "the teacher is the system" means that the model itself is intended to teach a repeatable pattern of responsibility.

## Genesis 10 and the 70 Concept

Genesis 10 is commonly described as presenting a table of nations or peoples. Traditional counts and identifications can vary because of textual and interpretive conventions, and the list includes peoples, regions, cities, and tribal names.

This project intentionally does not use those traditional names as labels for modern ethnic or national groups.

Instead, it uses three secular name-meaning headings:

## Symbolic, Not Ethnic

These categories are not claims that modern populations possess fixed psychological traits. They are a vocabulary for asking what a capability can contribute and what can happen when that capability is misdirected.

The framework's central question remains:

"What capability is present, what went wrong, what needs restoration, and where can that capability serve life rather than harm it?"
